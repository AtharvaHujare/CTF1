# Challenge Hints — The Hidden Parameter

These progressive hints are intended for CTF organizers and mentors to provide incrementally to players who get stuck.

---

### Hint 1 — Light

> The homepage isn't necessarily the entire application.

---

### Hint 2 — Medium

> The document viewer accepts more than just the filename you were given.

---

### Hint 3 — Strong

> Think about what `../` does when a server constructs a file path.
